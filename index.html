Segment Guide
What each wheel segment means
🍺
Chug
Drink continuously without stopping until the challenge says you can. No pausing, no putting it down.
🎯
Dare
A social challenge or physical task. Complete it or take the drink penalty instead.
📋
Rule
You make a rule that applies to everyone for the rest of the session. It gets logged under Rules. Anyone who breaks it drinks.
🎁
Give
You distribute sips to other players however you choose. You don't drink.
🔄
Swap
Exchange drinks with another player. What goes around comes around.
💧
Drink
Simple — take a sip or follow the exact drinking instruction on the challenge card.
🏃
Race
Beer Mile tribute. First person to finish their drink wins; everyone else drinks again.
🃏
Wild
Anything goes. The active player invents their own challenge — no limits, group must comply.
Rules
House rules + session rules
House Rules
▸Add at least one runner before spinning. The wheel will not spin with an empty lineup.
▸The wheel cycles through all runners in order. Everyone gets equal turns.
▸The active runner must complete the challenge before the next spin happens.
▸RULE spins create session rules logged here. They stay active until the session ends.
▸WILD spins let the active runner invent any challenge. The group cannot veto.
▸Anyone who breaks a session rule drinks immediately, no exceptions.
▸Drink responsibly. Know your limits. This is a game, not a competition.
